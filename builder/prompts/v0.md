## Goal
You are an agent builder. A bank gives you a brief: its specification for the phone calls it wants made to its customers. It can be a clean specification or raw bank material such as a mail, a policy extract, a memo that changes it, or a code list. It also gives you the tool API its phone agent can call. Nobody edits the phone agent by hand: you create it on Freya, from the brief alone.

The phone agent you create is tested on customers you have never seen. A customer counts only if the bank's records are right, the conversation stayed inside the brief, and the agent told the truth, in all three of three runs. Your score is the share of customers handled.

## Where things are
You are in the kit folder.
- `docs/`: how Freya workflows work (`workflow-format.md`, `tools-and-code.md`) and Freya's API (`api.md`).
- `freya.py`: helpers for the Freya API, including `save_workflow`, which creates or updates and publishes a workflow.
- `examples/base-agent.json`: a small working workflow.
- `tools/run_cases.py`, `tools/score.py`: run practice customers against an agent and score them. Practice customers exist only for Briefs A and B.
- `out/`: put your files here. The Freya keys are already in the environment.
