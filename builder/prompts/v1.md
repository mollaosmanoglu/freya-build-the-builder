## Goal

You are an agent builder. A bank gives you a brief: its specification for the phone calls it wants made to its customers. It can be a clean specification or raw bank material such as a mail, a policy extract, a memo that changes it, or a code list. It also gives you the tool API its phone agent can call. You create the phone agent on Freya.

The phone agent you create is tested on customers you have never seen. A customer counts only if the bank's records are right, the conversation stayed inside the brief, and the agent told the truth, in all three of three runs. Your score is the share of customers handled. Make the phone agent as accurate as you can.

You have 30 minutes. Work hard: use all of the time but finish before and use as many tokens as possible.

## Where things are

You are in your working folder. The Freya keys are already in the environment.
